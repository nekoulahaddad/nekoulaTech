# Project Business Value Plan

Goal: make every project page feel less like a technical demo and more like a business case. Each project should answer three things fast: what was broken, what we built, and what changed for the client.

## Recommended Site Structure

Use this block on every project page, ideally near the top after the hero: business problem, our solution, business impact, key product flows, technology stack. Homepage cards can stay short, but each card should include one sharp outcome sentence.

## Writing Rules

- Lead with money, control, speed, risk, transparency, or growth.
- Avoid only listing features; connect every feature to a business effect.
- Use numbers only where we are confident. If a number is not verified, write it as a draft metric.
- Keep the wording executive-friendly: owner, operations, revenue, leakage, compliance, conversion, retention.
- For Arabic copy, keep it close to how business owners speak, but polish it enough for a public website.

## InstituteTrack

- Problem: students were entering the institute without being properly registered. Attendance was not controlled, branch owners had no clear view of how many students were active in each branch, and both student and teacher attendance were hard to verify. This created revenue leakage, operational chaos, and room for internal abuse.
- Solution: we built an attendance ecosystem with student/staff registration, QR-based entry control, approval workflows, branch management, and admin analytics. The system made every visit traceable and gave management visibility into students, teachers, branches, and attendance patterns.
- Business impact: the institute reduced unauthorized access, exposed unregistered lessons, controlled teacher and student attendance, and increased revenue by around 150%.
- Suggested homepage line: controlled institute access and attendance with QR workflows, reducing leakage and increasing revenue by around 150%.

## RestManager

- Problem: the restaurant was losing money because many orders were not correctly registered in the POS. Some checks were closed without proper item registration, some were zeroed out, and management had no reliable way to detect theft, anomalies, or daily financial leakage.
- Solution: we connected POS data, Telegram workflows, reports, OCR invoices, anomaly detection, and owner-level dashboards. The system flags suspicious checks, compares purchasing data, escalates issues, and gives the owner daily, weekly, and monthly visibility.
- Business impact: the restaurant moved from losses to profit by reducing unregistered orders, detecting theft patterns, and giving the owner control over daily operations.
- Suggested homepage line: automated restaurant control through POS, Telegram, AI reports, and theft-risk scoring, turning operational leakage into measurable profit.

## FindCourier

- Problem: customers and independent couriers had no structured way to find each other, agree on routes, build trust, and track active deliveries. Deals depended on manual chats and weak reputation signals.
- Solution: we built a mobile courier marketplace with route-based listings, profiles, chat, active delivery states, reviews, favorites, push notifications, and deep links.
- Business impact: the platform turns scattered delivery requests into a repeatable marketplace flow, increasing successful matches and making trust visible through profiles and reviews.
- Suggested homepage line: converted informal delivery requests into a trusted courier marketplace with route search, chat, tracking, and reputation.

## KOVKERT

- Problem: construction and home-goods buyers needed to compare many products and sellers, while shops needed a scalable online sales channel beyond manual messages or offline discovery.
- Solution: we built a marketplace with catalog navigation, search, filters, product pages, basket, checkout, order tracking, favorites, reviews, and shop communication.
- Business impact: the product creates a digital sales channel for building-material suppliers, improves product discovery, and reduces friction from search to checkout.
- Suggested homepage line: launched a construction marketplace that improves product discovery, seller reach, and checkout conversion.

## KOVKERT Seller

- Problem: marketplace sellers needed one place to manage products, inventory, orders, partners, reviews, and customer chats. Without a seller workspace, operations become slow, fragmented, and hard to scale.
- Solution: we built a seller portal with catalog management, order processing, inventory controls, partner syncing, reviews, customer questions, and performance metrics.
- Business impact: sellers can process orders faster, keep stock data cleaner, respond to customers in one workspace, and scale marketplace operations with fewer manual errors.
- Suggested homepage line: gave sellers a daily operations cockpit for products, stock, orders, chats, and performance tracking.

## NovaChain

- Problem: crypto users need fast market discovery, clear trading context, wallet flows, and trust signals before moving funds. A weak interface lowers confidence and conversion.
- Solution: we built a crypto platform experience with markets, charts, notifications, launchpad flows, allocation rules, wallet switching, deposits, withdrawals, and multilingual UI.
- Business impact: the product improves user confidence and activation by making market exploration, launchpad participation, and fund movement clear and fast.
- Suggested homepage line: built a crypto experience focused on trust, speed, market clarity, and smooth launchpad participation.

## AppointControl

- Problem: medical teams needed to verify whether appointments and clinical actions matched protocols. Manual review made compliance slow, inconsistent, and hard to report to leadership.
- Solution: we built a secure analytics workspace for uploading protocols, filtering by doctor and ICD codes, flagging mismatches, visualizing status distribution, and exporting audit-ready reports.
- Business impact: the system reduces compliance risk, speeds up medical audits, and gives leadership a clearer view of doctor-level protocol adherence.
- Suggested homepage line: turned clinical protocol control into an auditable analytics workflow with anomaly flags and leadership reports.

## DesignersHub

- Problem: designers needed more than static portfolios: they needed discovery, community interaction, collaboration, and professional visibility in one place.
- Solution: we built a social platform with profiles, portfolio uploads, posts, likes, comments, notifications, follows, search by skill/category, and messaging.
- Business impact: the product increases creator visibility, supports collaboration, and turns portfolio browsing into an active community loop.
- Suggested homepage line: created a designer community where portfolios, discovery, engagement, and collaboration live in one product.

## ContentFactory

- Problem: teams needed several tools and long forms to prepare one post for multiple social networks, rewrite copy, generate descriptions, add tags, and publish everywhere.
- Solution: we built one-button content automation: upload one post, then AI adapts the copy, generates descriptions and tags, validates platform rules, and publishes to connected networks.
- Business impact: publishing time dropped from 2-3 minutes per post to a few seconds, manual effort fell by 85%, and one operator could manage 4x more channels.
- Suggested homepage line: automated multi-platform posting in seconds with AI copy, descriptions, tags, and platform adaptation.

## P300 Matrix

- Problem: the university team used a physical circuit that was hard to move, hard to reconfigure, and limited the number and variety of experiments they could run.
- Solution: we converted the setup into portable software with configurable colors, frequency, trial counts, 8 protocols, live logs, and export-ready outputs.
- Business impact: the team could run 3x more experiment variations, setup became 60% faster, and the tool became usable across labs without moving hardware.
- Suggested homepage line: converted a fixed lab circuit into portable software, enabling 3x more experiment variations.

## OpenAMRI

- Problem: readers face information overload and language barriers. News teams need better ways to package stories, summarize context, and keep users engaged.
- Solution: we built an AI-powered news platform with a multilingual feed, article pages, category browsing, conversational summaries, follow-up questions, and shareable story views.
- Business impact: the platform improves content discovery, increases reading depth, and makes news easier to understand across languages.
- Suggested homepage line: combined multilingual news, article pages, and AI chat to improve discovery, understanding, and engagement.

## SmileMe

- Problem: entertainment platforms struggle to understand real reactions. Likes and watch time do not always show what genuinely makes users laugh.
- Solution: we built a mobile comedy experience with exclusive creator content, smile detection, reaction scoring, personalized recommendations, follows, saves, and trend updates.
- Business impact: the product can improve retention and content relevance by recommending videos based on real emotional response, not only manual engagement.
- Suggested homepage line: used smile-based AI personalization to match viewers with comedy content they actually react to.

## Questions To Validate

- Which projects have confirmed business metrics we can publish?
- Can we publicly mention theft, corruption, or revenue leakage, or should we soften it to "operational leakage"?
- Should each project page include a new "Business impact" section, or should we rewrite the existing hero and overview only?
- Do we want the primary language for business cases to be English, Russian, Arabic, or all three?
